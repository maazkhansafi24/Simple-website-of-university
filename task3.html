About Student Activities

The UET Mardan Student Activities Portal is designed to provide students with information about university events and activities. Students can check upcoming programs, view event schedules and register for activities through this portal.

The portal encourages students to participate in academic, technical, sports and social activities and helps them develop skills outside the classroom.

Upcoming Events

Check out some of the upcoming activities at UET Mardan.

Tech Seminar

A seminar where students can learn about modern technology and career opportunities.

Programming Competition

Students will solve programming problems and compete with other participants.

Sports Day

A fun sports event where students can participate in different games and activities.

Web Development Workshop

A practical workshop covering basic web development concepts and tools.
